## ✨ &nbsp;About Me
**Hi! I'm Scarllett Lins.** 👋

I'm a **QA Analyst** with a strong background in software engineering. By combining my expertise in software testing with **Cybersecurity**, I am actively building a solid career path aiming to become a **Pentester**. 

With over 7 years of experience spanning tech, e-commerce, and system architecture, my daily mission is to ensure software quality, API resilience, and security. I hold a degree in Systems Analysis and Development and I am currently pursuing postgraduate degrees in **Cybersecurity** and **DevOps**, diving deep into test automation, vulnerability mapping, and AppSec practices.

Beyond the corporate servers and security protocols, my passion for gaming takes over. I am an avid gamer and an indie developer. This creative drive for world-building led me to found my own indie studio, **[INKD GAMES](https://inkdgames.com)**, where I bring my pixel-art projects to life and frequently participate in game jams.
